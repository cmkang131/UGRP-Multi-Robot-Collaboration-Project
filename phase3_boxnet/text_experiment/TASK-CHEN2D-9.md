## Q1. `failure_causes.py RUNDIR [RUNDIR ...]` (read-only; must accept run folders of versions 6, 7 and 8 and of this version; it may be given several folders that differ in `max_output_tokens` or history settings and must then group by those settings instead of refusing)
For every episode decide ONE cause, by fixed rules written in the docstring and the report:
- `completed`: strict success.
- `output_limit`: the episode ended with the label `failure output limit`, OR it ended otherwise unsuccessfully and its last planning call was cut off at the output limit on every attempt.
- `context`: the episode ended with `Syntactic Error`, or with `failure over query time limit` / the call ceiling, or as not strict because boxes were left, AND the record shows the input-side pattern: at least one invalid-plan repair (authors' feedback "not in the doable action list" or the JSON-format feedback) in the last planning iteration, or the same invalid action proposed again after feedback, or an executed plan that repeats a move pattern of period 1 or 2 for at least 4 consecutive steps (a box moved back and forth) before the end. Record which of the three sub-patterns applied.
- `wrong_delivery`: not strict only because of a wrong delivery (the news-related failures and drop-off misuse), with no output-limit or context pattern.
- `other`: anything else (model service errors, hallucination label without the patterns above...).
Also per episode: number of cut-off attempts and cut-off calls; number of repair calls and of planning iterations that needed at least one repair; share of planning iterations whose FIRST proposed plan was invalid (cleaner than counting repair calls); mean and maximum prompt tokens of plan-making calls; prompt tokens at the first invalid plan; completion and reasoning tokens per plan-making call; steps executed.
Output: a CSV per episode and a table grouped by (model, max_output_tokens, history setting, size, framework, condition): episodes, completed, output_limit, context (with the three sub-patterns), wrong_delivery, other, as counts and shares; cut-off calls share; first-plan-invalid share for early steps (1 to 4) and late steps (9+); mean prompt tokens early and late. A second table "who is ahead": per (model, size, max_output_tokens, history setting, condition) the completed share of CMAS and DMAS and which is higher, so that flips along the output limit or the history setting are visible.

## Q2. A context-length knob for the authors' history (default = today's behaviour byte for byte)
- `--history-window N`: the token limit of the authors' state-action history window (their `input_prompt_token_limit`, 3000 for BoxNet1; today counted with the approximate counter). Applies to the frameworks that have a history in the authors' code (DMAS, HMAS-1, HMAS-2). `0` means no history.
- `--history-method authors | dialogue | none`: `authors` = `_w_only_state_action_history` (today), `dialogue` = the authors' `_w_all_dialogue_history`, `none` = `_wo_any_dialogue_history`. Use the authors' own code paths; do not write new history logic.
- `--cmas-history off | on` (default off = the authors' CMAS branch, which shows no history): `on` gives the central planner the same state-action history text the other frameworks get under the selected window. This is our addition, for asking whether the leader also suffers when its input grows; report it as a deviation.
- The run folder records the three settings; the summary and the other tools group by them or refuse to mix, as fits each tool.
